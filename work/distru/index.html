More work Distru · Product Designer · 2021–2022

Distru: product design and systems

Distru is enterprise resource planning and logistics software for the cannabis industry, covering CRM, inventory management, order fulfillment, and state-specific track-and-trace compliance. As one of three product designers, I worked with the product manager and engineering team across feature projects, marketing, and a new design system.

Role
Product designer, one of three
Team
PM, engineering, Customer Success, Sales and Marketing
Timeline
November 2021 – June 2022
Highlight
Self-guided onboarding cut onboarding time and support calls by 50%
  • Live Menu
  • Client onboarding
  • Dynamic Bills of Materials
  • Recurring and cloning tasks
  • Marketing and branding
  • Design system

Project 1Live Menu

Using each client's inventory database, Distru can generate multiple live sales menus, each customizable. The feature had a lot of room for improvement, and the backlog of customer requests and feedback was a good place to start.

  • Reviewed and prioritized customer requests and feedback in Productboard with the PM
  • Ran a visual and UX audit of the existing feature, and scoped the PRD with the PM
  • Designed wireframes and iterated on them with the PM and Customer Success
  • Reviewed the PRD and designs with engineering to uncover technical constraints and plan implementation
  • Handed off to engineers, QA tested each release, and checked in weekly on status
  • Coordinated each release with Customer Success, Sales and Marketing, and demoed the feature to align client communication

Project 2Client onboarding

Onboarding is a client's first contact with the product, and the existing workflow needed Customer Success to be hands-on throughout: account creation and setup, API configuration, data entry, and going live. Stakeholders wanted a more streamlined process, so clients could be more self-sufficient and Customer Success could free up bandwidth.

  • Sat in on client onboarding calls to understand user needs
  • Walked through the process with Customer Success to understand the business needs
  • Ran a UI audit and mapped user flows in flowcharts to synthesize findings
  • Brainstormed solutions with the product team, then designed and iterated on wireframes with the PM and Customer Success
  • Reviewed the PRD and designs with engineering, then finalized and handed off
50%less onboarding time and fewer support calls

Project 3Dynamic Bills of Materials

For manufacturers, assembling a product isn't always a simple equation of combining ingredients and materials. They need an assembly process that is both efficient and dynamic, based on inventory levels and ingredient expiration dates.

  • Walked through the existing Bill of Materials feature with the PM and audited it
  • Scoped the PRD with the PM; designed and iterated on wireframes with the PM and Customer Success
  • Reviewed with engineering, handed off, QA tested each release, and coordinated launches with Customer Success, Sales and Marketing

Project 4Recurring and cloning tasks

For manufacturers and distributors, keeping to a schedule is vital, and each employee's schedule is full of tasks that need to be done on time. Letting managers assign tasks accurately and efficiently is key to minimizing errors.

  • Scoped and planned with the PM, and audited the existing feature
  • Ran a competitive analysis of task and schedule management apps
  • Ideated solutions, reviewed them with stakeholders and engineering, then finalized and handed off

Project 5Marketing strategy and branding

First impressions are everything. In SaaS, aesthetics can feel homogenized, and brand identity needs to stand out. Working with the Marketing team, I designed promotional materials for events, in-app splash illustrations and icons, marketing site layouts and illustrations, and social media and blog illustrations.

Project 6Design system

As Distru shipped features over time, inconsistencies between pages and components accumulated. When the design team grew from one to three designers, it was the right time to build a design system. We met weekly, set goals and action items, and worked through:

  • An app-wide visual audit, then analysis and synthesis of the findings
  • Comparing and adopting a Figma MUI library
  • Building shared components with engineering in Figma and Storybook
  • Prioritizing incremental UI updates against the library specs
  • Communicating each release across teams with recorded demos
