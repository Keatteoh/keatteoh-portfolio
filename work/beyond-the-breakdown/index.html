Sundance 2021 Visual and UX/UI Design

Beyond the Breakdown

Beyond the Breakdown (opens in new tab) is a browser-based interactive experience that premiered on January 30, 2021 at the Sundance Film Festival (opens in new tab) (New Frontier), created by artists Tony Patrick, Lauren Lee McCarthy, and Grace Lee. It is an AI-moderated chatroom where groups of strangers share and discuss their visions and values for building a post-pandemic world.
