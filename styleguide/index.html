01 · ColorTokens

All color comes from seven tokens on :root, redefined for dark mode. Tints are mixed from them with color-mix() rather than added as new colors.

Text on the accent uses --accent-ink (#ffffff light, #0d1020 dark). Tinted panels use the accent at 10% over the surface, with a 30% accent border.

03 · ShapeRadius and spacing

8 · focus

18 · panels
24 · cards
999 · pills
  • Page: .wrap is min(1120px, 100% − 32px), a 16px gutter on phones.
  • Reading column: 700px for text; .wide breaks out to 1040px for figures.
  • Rhythm: sections start 72px apart (56px on phones); blocks sit 28–40px apart; card padding is 20–24px.
  • Elevation: flat. Cards use a 1px --line border instead of shadows; only the homepage portrait has one.
